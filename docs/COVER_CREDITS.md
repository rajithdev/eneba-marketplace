# Cover picture credits

Written by `scripts/catalog/paint-covers.mjs` from `scripts/catalog/painted-covers.json`. Do not edit by hand.

Every game has a painted cover in `web/public/covers/art/`. None is a
publisher's official artwork.

## Built from public-domain pictures

3 covers are built from pictures that are in the public domain or released
under CC0, found on Wikimedia Commons. Each was cropped to the card's shape
(600 x 800), shaded at the top and the foot, and given the game's title in
type. They are not derived from the games' artwork.

The licence in each row is the one Wikimedia Commons states on the file's
page, read on 18 November 2026. This is a cautious reading, not legal advice.

## Drawn by the script

6 covers are gift cards and subscriptions drawn entirely by the script, with
no logo: Steam Wallet Gift Card 20 EUR, Nintendo eShop Card 25 EUR, PlayStation Store Gift Card 50 USD, Xbox Game Pass Ultimate 1 Month, PlayStation Plus Essential 12 Months, EA Play 12 Months.

## Supplied by the author

The remaining 25 covers were made and supplied by the author as finished
pictures, and scaled to 600 x 800: Elden Ring, Cyberpunk 2077, The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, Red Dead Redemption 2, Grand Theft Auto V, Minecraft, Hogwarts Legacy, Baldur's Gate 3, Stardew Valley, Hades, Hollow Knight, Terraria, DOOM Eternal, Resident Evil 4, God of War, Forza Horizon 5, Sea of Thieves, EA Sports FC 25, It Takes Two, Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice, Sid Meier's Civilization VI, Helldivers 2, Mario Kart 8 Deluxe, The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom, Cyberpunk 2077: Phantom Liberty.

## Fonts

The titles are set in fonts that ship with macOS (Didot, DIN Condensed,
Rockwell, American Typewriter, Avenir Next). Only the rendered picture is
published, not the fonts.
